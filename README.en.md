# BetterLyrics Lite

BetterLyrics Lite is a Windows lyrics player based on BetterLyrics. It keeps two window styles, playback-source integration, synced lyrics, and listening reports.

[Download the Windows x64 installer](https://github.com/DrivingGodJ/BetterLyrics-HDR/releases/latest) · [Install and build guide](docs/INSTALL.LITE.en.md)

- **Standard mode** fills the left side with full-height artwork and reserves at least half of the window width for lyrics. The track title and artist adapt to the window size.
- **Taskbar mode** shows the current lyric in the taskbar and updates its background colors with the album art. Both window styles can be customized in settings.
- **Synced lyrics** follow playback with karaoke highlighting. With Windows HDR enabled, turn on HDR lyric highlights and glow in the window settings.
- **Listening reports** are generated from your playback history and available from the tray.

Opening the app manually loads your default lyrics window. Enable **Start silently at sign-in** under **Settings → Lyrics Window Management** to load it in the background at Windows sign-in. The window follows its auto-hide settings. Right-click the tray icon to open settings, switch windows, or view listening reports.

The [previous BetterLyrics HDR release](https://github.com/DrivingGodJ/BetterLyrics-HDR/releases/tag/v1.3.553-hdr.1) is still available. BetterLyrics Lite is based on [jayfunc/BetterLyrics](https://github.com/jayfunc/BetterLyrics) and distributed under the [GPL-3.0 license](LICENSE).
